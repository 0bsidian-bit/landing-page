This website

lokeshtewari.uk is the personal website of Dr. Lokesh Tewari. It is a non-commercial project: it carries no advertising, sells nothing, has no paid features and earns no income. It offers no medical advice or consultations.

The site hosts 0bsidian, a free browser homepage and focus timer made and maintained by Dr. Tewari. It offers a search bar and shortcuts for everyday browsing, and a pomodoro timer for focused work, set on NASA's Astronomy Picture of the Day.

0bsidian needs no account and collects no personal data. Anything a visitor saves, such as tasks or settings, stays in their own browser. The source code is public on GitHub.
