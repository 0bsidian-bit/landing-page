          0bsidian

          A homepage and pomodoro timer set on a new picture every day, from NASA's astronomy archive or Bing. Search the web and keep your shortcuts on the search view. Switch to focus and, once a session starts, the picture stays unobserved, grey and out of focus, resolving into colour as you work. Finish the session and you see all of it.

          privacy

          Tasks, settings and history are saved in this browser and nowhere else. There's no sign-up, analytics, ads or cookies. Pictures are fetched by this site's server and passed on to you, so your browser never connects to NASA or Microsoft.
